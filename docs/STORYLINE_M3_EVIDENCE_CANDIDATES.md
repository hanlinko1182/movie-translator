# M3-01 Evidence analysis candidates

M3-01 adds pure versioned contracts and a deterministic validator in `lib/storyline/evidence-analysis/`. It does not install a provider, worker, route, migration, receipt issuer, or publication path. The M2 runtime and spending gate remain disabled. The validator returns `PROVISIONAL_CANDIDATE`, never `SOURCE_VERIFIED` or approved Studio content.

## Contract and grounding

`story-evidence-candidate-v1` carries an exact M2 task ID, generation and recipe hash; project, movie, Studio, source snapshot and dependency hashes; fixed profile and model metadata; findings; competing interpretations; and open questions for insufficient evidence. The server must supply the authoritative M1 snapshot, M2 recipe identity, task-generation identity and fixed profile separately. Candidate fields cannot establish those identities on their own. The pure check does not prove that the supplied server inputs are persisted or currently authorized; a future M2 service must load and revalidate them through its restricted database and source-currentness gates.

Findings separate `OBSERVED` from `INFERRED`. Observed dialogue must repeat an exact Chinese quotation. Observed action/behavior must repeat a frozen M1 behavior observation, and observed relationship material must repeat a frozen relationship observation. This is a conservative structural rule that excludes paraphrased observed claims at this stage. Conflict, decision, consequence, motivation and trait interpretations require inference treatment and source review. An inferred candidate must have one or more same-subject observed bases, a qualification, a non-certain uncertainty label and no higher confidence than its basis. Competing interpretations are explicit pairs of qualified inferred findings; neither is elevated to fact. Open questions can describe contradictory or insufficient evidence without manufacturing a positive claim. The contract does not include diagnoses.

Every anchor must resolve to the exact Chinese transcript, scene, segment and task window. Character claims use their own frozen character evidence allowances. Relationship claims require the exact relationship and both endpoint characters, plus relationship evidence. Captured observations must match their frozen owner and source row IDs. A Chinese quote must be a literal substring of the frozen Chinese segment. Duplicate evidence references, forged IDs, cross-movie references, invalid times/scenes and unsupported owner references are rejected. Confidence is capped by character/relationship evidence and uncertain identities stay LOW. These rules prove structural grounding, not Chinese semantic truth or psychological correctness.

Myanmar ALL_CURRENT context is optional and advisory. Every referenced row must be admitted by M1 and match its exact row-context hash and the finding's source segment. Approval, manual edit provenance and QC state are retained within the immutable M1 snapshot and hash. They do not expand evidence ownership or source verification. Missing, stale and invalid rows cannot be cited. A change to Chinese text, source media, translation, QC or approval changes source/recipe identity and invalidates an old candidate binding.

The validator rejects unknown fields, malformed Unicode, excess size, duplicate identifiers and unsupported contract versions. The candidate version must match the immutable M2 profile's response schema version, and serialized bytes must fit both the candidate ceiling and that profile's response-byte limit. Inferred qualifications must contain one of the existing cautious Myanmar markers; an UNKNOWN relationship cannot claim confidence above LOW. It preserves exact Unicode text and returns canonical SHA-256 candidate identity. Array order is significant; identical payloads produce identical hashes. It neither normalizes nor rewrites source text. Provider and model IDs are checked against the supplied fixed M2 profile, but no actual provider response is fetched.

## M2 handoff and limits

A future M3-02 service must accept results only after the existing M2 restricted checkpoint, acknowledged send, result and UNKNOWN rules. It must load the persisted task/generation and immutable source/recipe/profile, run this validator, and keep the resulting candidate separate from M1 script versions and trusted human verification receipts. A valid candidate cannot approve a script, issue a source-verification receipt, or count an event toward supported duration on its own. Human source comparison remains required.

M3-02 also needs a server-owned policy for semantic review of paraphrases and a fixed provider response schema. The current exact-quote/exact-observation rule intentionally rejects semantic paraphrases because a pure validator cannot prove them. No runtime or paid activation is authorized by M3-01.
